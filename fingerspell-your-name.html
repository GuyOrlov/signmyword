Names & fingerspelling

Fingerspell your name in BSL or ASL

Enter a name in SignMyWord and see each letter as British Sign Language or American Sign Language fingerspelling. This is useful for learning the manual alphabet and practising how a written name is spelled visually.

Try in BSL Try in ASL

How it works

Type the name, choose BSL or ASL, and the generator displays one fingerspelling image for each letter. You can then share a link or create a picture of the result.

Fingerspelling and sign names are different

SignMyWord spells a written name using the manual alphabet. It does not invent or assign a sign name. Sign names are a separate part of Deaf communities and sign-language use.
