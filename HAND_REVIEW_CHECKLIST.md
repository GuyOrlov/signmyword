# SignMyWord hand-image review checklist

This checklist supports fluent BSL and ASL review of every A–Z hand image before accuracy is described as verified.

## Review criteria
- Handshape is accurate and recognisable.
- Palm/finger orientation is clear.
- Two-handed BSL letters show both hands correctly.
- Any movement needed to understand a letter is explained.
- Image crop does not remove important fingers, wrists or movement cues.
- Alt text describes the letter without claiming full sign-language translation.
- Reviewer name/initials, date and notes are recorded.
